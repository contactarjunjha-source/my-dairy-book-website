Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Last updated: 19 October 2026

My Dairy Book (“My Dairy Book”, “the app”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a dairy management application for dairy owners, collection centres, milk sellers, delivery businesses and farms. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the My Dairy Book Android app and this website.

By using My Dairy Book, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Who we are

My Dairy Book is developed and operated by the My Dairy Book team (“the developer”). For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact us at mydairybook01@gmail.com.

2. Information you give us

When you create an account and use the app, you may provide:

  • Account information: your mobile number, one-time password verification, and Firebase user ID.
  • Business profile: your name, dairy name, phone number, address, village, business type, GSTIN, UPI ID, currency, milk unit and logo.
  • Farmer records: name, code, phone numbers, address, village, animal type, rate method, bank account, IFSC, UPI ID, notes, photo and opening balance.
  • Customer records: name, code, phone, address, customer type, rate, delivery schedule, route, GSTIN, credit limit, payment terms, notes, photo and opening balance.
  • Business transactions: milk collections, sales, purchases, payments, expenses, stock movements, delivery skips, daily closings, shift closings, staff records and rate charts.
  • Photos and files: profile photos, product images, expense receipts and rate-chart photos you choose to attach.
  • Voice input: short spoken entries you choose to dictate. Speech recognition is provided by your device's speech service; we do not store raw audio on our servers.
  • Support messages: information you send us by email or through support requests.
  • Payment information: the plan you buy, its status and expiry, and a payment or order reference. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Google Play or Razorpay and are not stored by us.

3. Information collected automatically

We may collect limited technical information needed to operate and secure the app, such as app version, device type, operating system, crash diagnostics and basic usage events. Firebase and Google Play services may process this information on our behalf. We do not use advertising identifiers for personalised advertising.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • create and secure your account and verify your mobile number;
  • store and display your dairy records, ledgers, reports and bills;
  • calculate milk rates, balances, profits and other business figures;
  • generate PDF bills, receipts and reports that you ask for;
  • activate, renew and manage your paid plan;
  • provide customer support and respond to your requests;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and improve the app;
  • detect fraud, abuse and security incidents; and
  • comply with applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your dairy records for targeted advertising, and we do not use them to train public artificial-intelligence models.

5. Legal bases for processing

Where required, we process your information because it is necessary to perform our contract with you (providing the app), because you have consented (for example, camera, microphone or photo access), because we have a legitimate interest in operating and securing the service, or because we must comply with the law.

6. How your information is shared

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: authentication, database hosting, storage and infrastructure. Firebase Authentication processes your mobile number for OTP sign-in. Firebase Data Connect and Google Cloud SQL store your business records.
  • Google Play services: app distribution, crash diagnostics and platform services.
  • Google Play Billing and Razorpay: process your subscription or one-time payment and tell us whether your plan is active. They handle payment credentials under their own privacy policies; we receive only the plan, status, expiry and a payment reference.
  • Google ML Kit: on-device text recognition for scanned rate charts and documents. The image is processed on your device.
  • Your device's speech recognition service: converts your voice to text. The provider's own privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Sharing you initiate: when you share a bill, PDF, CSV or report using your phone's share sheet, the selected app or person receives that file.
  • Legal requirements: when we believe disclosure is required by law, regulation, court order or to protect rights, safety and security.

We may also transfer information as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

7. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate, including India and other countries where Google Cloud/Firebase maintains infrastructure. We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

8. Data retention

We retain your account and business records for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you request deletion, we remove your account and associated business records from active systems, except where we must retain certain information for legal, tax, fraud-prevention or security purposes. Backup copies are deleted on our normal backup rotation. Payment and subscription records may be kept for the period required by tax, accounting and fraud-prevention law.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted HTTPS connections, Firebase Authentication, restricted database access and account-level data separation. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your mobile number and OTP safe and tell us immediately if you suspect unauthorised access.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit most information directly in the app.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and business data immediately in the app from More → Settings → Delete Account. You can also request deletion by email. See our Data Deletion page.
  • Permissions: you can revoke camera, microphone, photo or notification permissions in your phone settings.
  • Marketing: we do not send marketing emails or SMS. Service-related messages may still be sent.
  • Complaints: contact us at mydairybook01@gmail.com and we will try to resolve your concern.

11. Children's privacy

My Dairy Book is intended for adults who own or manage a dairy business. It is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us so we can delete it.

12. Cookies and this website

This website is a simple information site. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent. See Cookies below.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be communicated in the app or by email where appropriate. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
