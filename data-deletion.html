Account

Data Deletion

Applies to the My Dairy Book Android app and the data linked to your account.

You can delete your My Dairy Book account directly inside the app, or ask us to do it by email if you cannot sign in. This page explains what is deleted, what may be retained, and how long it takes.

How to delete your account

1
Delete it in the app (fastest)

Open My Dairy Book, go to More → Settings → Delete Account, type DELETE and confirm. This permanently removes your account and all data linked to it. For your security, the app may ask you to sign in again first.

2
Or email us

If you cannot sign in, send a message to mydairybook01@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my My Dairy Book account”. Include the mobile number you use to sign in (with country code). Do not send your OTP or any password.

3
We verify email requests and confirm

We may ask you to confirm an email request from the registered number. Once verified, we will start the deletion process and confirm by email.

What will be deleted

  • Your sign-in account and account profile.
  • Your dairy/business profile, including logo and UPI ID.
  • Farmer, customer, supplier and staff records you created.
  • Milk collections, sales, purchases, payments, expenses, stock movements and delivery records.
  • Rate charts, bills, reports, closing records and attached photos.

What may be retained

We may retain a limited record of the deletion request and basic transaction logs where required by law, for tax or accounting obligations, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to resolve disputes. Retained data is limited to what is necessary and kept only for the required period. Backup copies are removed as our backup rotation completes.

Timeline

In-app deletion is immediate. Your account and data are removed when you confirm. Email requests are verified and completed within 30 days; if we need more time, we will tell you why. You will receive a confirmation when an email request is complete.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual farmers, customers, products, entries, payments, bills and other records inside the app. Deleting an individual record removes it from your active account. Some linked records may remain for accounting consistency; contact us if you need help.

Before you delete

Deletion is permanent. Please export or print any bills, ledgers or reports you need before deleting your account. If you only want to stop using the app, sign out from Settings; your data will remain until you delete your account or ask us to do it.

Deletion requests

Email: mydairybook01@gmail.com
We usually respond within 2 business days.
