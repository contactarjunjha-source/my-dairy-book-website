Milk collection

Record morning and evening collections by farmer, shift, milk type, quantity, FAT, SNF and CLR in seconds.

Smart rate charts

Set FAT-only, FAT + SNF or FAT + CLR charts. Get the right rate for every reading, rounded your way.

Common questions

Good to know
before you start.

Still have a question? Write to us at mydairybook01@gmail.com.

Is My Dairy Book free?

Yes. The app is currently free to download and use. If paid plans are introduced, the price will be shown before you buy.

Does it work without internet?

You need an internet connection to sign in and save your records to your account. Some parts of the app keep recent data on your phone, but a connection is recommended for reliable syncing.

Can I use it for both cow and buffalo milk?

Yes. You can set separate rates and rate charts for cow and buffalo milk, and choose FAT-only, FAT + SNF or FAT + CLR pricing.

Can I print or share bills?

Yes. Create monthly customer bills with your logo and UPI QR, then share or print them as a PDF from your phone.

What happens to my data if I delete my account?

Your account and linked business records are removed from active systems. Limited information may be kept where required by law. See the Data Deletion page, linked in the footer.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi, Haryanvi, Rajasthani/Marwari, Bengali, Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, Odia, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, Malayalam and Urdu.

Does the app show ads?

No. My Dairy Book does not show third-party ads, and we do not sell your business data.
