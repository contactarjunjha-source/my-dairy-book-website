Billing

Billing & Refunds

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Free plan

My Dairy Book is free to download. Every dairy gets five free entries in total. A collection entry, milk sale, product sale or supplier purchase each counts as one entry. You can use the app without paying until those five entries are used.

Paid plans

After the free entries are used, a paid plan unlocks unlimited entries and all app features. The current plans are:

  • Monthly: ₹139 per month.
  • Yearly: ₹1,199 per year.

Prices are shown in Indian Rupees. The price and billing period are always shown before you confirm a payment. We may change prices in the future; a change will never apply to a payment you have already completed.

Google Play subscriptions

If you pay through Google Play, Google Play Billing processes the payment. Google Play subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless you cancel before the renewal date. You can view, manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. Google's payment terms and refund policy apply. You can also request a refund through Google Play Help.

Razorpay payments

If you pay through Razorpay, Razorpay processes the payment using UPI, cards, net banking or other methods shown at checkout. Razorpay payments are one-time payments for the selected period and do not renew automatically. To stay unlocked, renew before the expiry date shown in the app. We do not receive or store your full card number, UPI PIN or bank credentials; Razorpay handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Refunds and cancellations

Google Play purchases are refunded according to Google Play's refund policy. For Razorpay payments, contact us at mydairybook01@gmail.com within 7 days of payment if you were charged twice, charged in error, or could not use the paid features. We will review the request and, where a refund is approved, return the amount to the original payment method. Refunds may take 5–7 business days to appear after approval.

Cancelling a Google Play subscription stops future renewals. It does not automatically refund the current period, but you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Questions

If you have a billing question or believe you were charged in error, contact us at mydairybook01@gmail.com with your order number or the mobile number linked to your account and we will help you investigate.
